KNOW YOUR EYES

Simple habits and timely checks can protect vision.

The eyes are among our most important sensory organs. Regular care and proper examination help identify refractive errors and other eye problems early.

01Blurred distance or near vision may be caused by Myopia, Hypermetropia or Astigmatism.
02Screen use can contribute to eye strain and dryness; regular breaks and proper ergonomics help.
03Children’s vision problems should be identified early to support normal learning and visual development.
04Diabetes, high blood pressure and sudden visual changes may require comprehensive eye evaluation.

WHEN IT MAY BE URGENT

Some eye symptoms should not wait.

Seek urgent eye care for sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, significant trauma, chemical injury, new flashes with many floaters, a curtain-like shadow, or a red eye with major vision change.

RIGHT SPECIALIST

Which eye-care professional may be appropriate?

The correct specialist depends on the suspected condition. A comprehensive eye examination can help guide referral when the diagnosis is not yet known.

R

Power / glasses

Optometrist for primary refractive assessment; Ophthalmologist if ocular disease is suspected.

C

Cataract

Ophthalmologist, especially a Cataract / Anterior Segment specialist.

G

Glaucoma

Glaucoma Specialist or Ophthalmologist.

Rt

Retina

Retina Specialist / Ophthalmologist.

Co

Cornea

Cornea Specialist / Ophthalmologist.

P

Children / squint

Pediatric Ophthalmologist or Strabismus specialist for squint, amblyopia or visual-development concerns.

O

Eyelid / orbit

Oculoplasty Specialist / Ophthalmologist for eyelid, lacrimal-system and orbital problems.

!

Trauma / emergency

Urgent Ophthalmologist or Eye Emergency service for severe trauma, sudden vision loss or chemical injury.

EYE CARE IN BANGLADESH

Know what to consider before choosing eye care.

Bangladesh offers eye-care services through government hospitals, medical college hospitals, private hospitals and specialized eye-care institutions. Available services vary by facility and location.

  • Check clinician qualifications and the institution’s reputation.
  • Consider diagnostic facilities and specialist availability.
  • Ask about follow-up arrangements when ongoing monitoring is needed.
  • Do not start treatment only from online information without appropriate examination.
Practical guidance

Not every complex eye condition requires treatment abroad.

Many common and complex eye conditions can be diagnosed, treated and surgically managed in Bangladesh. Overseas referral depends on the condition and the treating specialist’s advice.

FINAL NOTE

The right test, at the right time, matters.

Eye problems should not all be treated the same way. From refractive error to glaucoma, cataract, retina or corneal disease, each condition may require different tests and different treatment. For serious or sudden symptoms, do not rely only on online information—seek qualified eye care.
